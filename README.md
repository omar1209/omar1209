### Research

**Vancouver's rental market** (Feb – Apr 2025). Cleaned and merged CMHC housing data in Python and SQL and fit a log-transformed regression of rents on vacancy rates, controlling for region, unit type and market density, with robust standard errors and robustness checks. The relationship came out positive, which I traced to market segmentation and luxury housing.

**Military spending and economic growth** (capstone, Jan – Apr 2025). Panel analysis of 180 countries from 1960 to 2019 in STATA with fixed effects, using World Bank and Penn World Table data. In an augmented Solow model, a 1% increase in military spending reduced GDP per capita growth by 0.092%, with effects 3.7x stronger in high-income countries.
